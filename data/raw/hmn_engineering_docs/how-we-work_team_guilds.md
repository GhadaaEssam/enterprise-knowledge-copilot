# Guilds

Guilds are cross-functional, cross-team groups with a specific focus and continuing goal. This focus and goal is directly related to the delivery of projects, and contributes to an increased capacity to produce work. Read more about guilds on the [announcement post](https://updates.hmn.md/2018/08/21/introducing-guilds/).

Guilds cut across many teams, and incorporate engineers, project managers, and anyone else involved in delivery, whether client services or otherwise.

[Please refer to the Company Handbook for a list of currently active Guilds and details of their meeting times and additional resources](https://handbook.hmn.md/working-here/guilds/).
